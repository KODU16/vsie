# ![banner](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/153177a4191976f449f9b1fd7785558fb7d3b884_0.webp)Aeronautics: InterStellar Expansion

> **Build a starship, Link its systems. Command it as one block.**

Welcome to **AeroIE**, an addon for **Create: Aeronautics** that focuses only on fully controllable spacecraft built for flight, combat, defense, and long-range travel.

At its heart is the **Control Seat**. Connect a ship's engines, weapons, sensors, shields, and storage into one integrated network—without tedious thruster placement or UI-heavy micromanagement.

![Control Seat HUD](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/aa259f3a619f1f4b5eeee6d94ee2d9d5682cf73b.png)

## Key Features

- **Integrated flight control** with automatic thrust allocation and vector thrusters
- **Ship-mounted weapons**, guided missiles, automated turrets, and CIWS
- **Radar and IFF systems** for detecting and identifying targets
- **Modular shields** for protecting vessels of different shapes and sizes
- **Fuel tanks, batteries, and ammunition systems** designed for moving ships
- **Warp travel** across long distances within the same dimension
- **Electromagnetic rails** for launching and accelerating entire vessels

and with more exciting peripherals incoming!

AeroIE is designed to make complex spacecraft systems **reliable, interconnected, and easy to control**, while leaving the ship's appearance, layout, and purpose entirely up to you.
